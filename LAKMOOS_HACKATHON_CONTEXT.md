# Lakmoos AI — Company & Hackathon Context

## About Lakmoos AI
- Czech AI startup (VUT FIT spin-off, 2023), VC-backed, award-winning
- Core product: replaces traditional surveys with AI synthetic respondents (neuro-symbolic models)
- Simulates how target audience groups think, feel, and behave — without asking real people
- Claims 98% accuracy vs real surveys; GDPR compliant; serves enterprise clients
- Goal: replace 20% of traditional surveys by 2030, saving $30B in research costs and 35Bn hours of fieldwork annually
- Key pain point (from their own blog): synthetic panels drift from reality over time because they're trained on historical data, not current media

## Their Business Model
- Clients define a research topic + target audience
- Lakmoos generates synthetic panel responses instantly (no recruitment, no fieldwork)
- Output: structured market research insights, audience reaction predictions

## Hackathon — Media Intelligence (19–20 March 2026, VUT FIT room C228)
- Duration: 24 hours (starts 19.3. 14:00, ends 20.3. 14:00)
- Teams: max 5 people
- Backend only (no design/frontend required)
- Language: Python >= 3.11
- Challenge: Design and implement a backend service that:
  1. Collects media content from selected sources
  2. Produces structured summaries
  3. Supplies analytical insights to capture current events and trends
- Evaluation criteria: NLP/ML application, clear technical decisions, innovative approach, scalability
- Prize: 5000 CZK team cash prize + internship opportunity at Lakmoos

## What Judges Want to See
- Thoughtful NLP/ML usage (not just keyword matching)
- Well-justified architecture decisions
- Scalable system design
- Connection to their core business problem (calibrating synthetic panels to real-world media)
